8.a

Vector Explainability

SkillBridge vectorizes skills beyond keyword matching, understanding that “vendor management” and “stakeholder coordination” are related capabilities. AI extraction pulls 30–60 skills per resume, including inferred skills from context. PII is stripped before matching. Every result includes a GPT-generated explanation of what aligned, what's missing, and how to close the gap.

8.b

Hiring Manager Portal

Hiring managers see anonymized, skill-first candidate profiles with match scores and gap analysis before any conversation. Aggregate skill distributions surface common gaps across candidates, so managers enter interviews with realistic expectations. Tokenized identities protect privacy until mutual opt-in.

8.c

Career Roadmaps & Skills Portfolio

AI-generated roadmaps break transitions into phased plans with milestones, timelines, and prerequisites. The Skills Portfolio organizes capabilities into four-tier learning modules (Fundamentals through Mastery). Success Patterns show how employees in similar roles have transitioned, including common skills and average timelines.

AI Differentiators

SkillBridge is different because of how we use AI

Behavioral Success Modeling

Six categories of performance data drive real benchmarks per level. Recommendations are grounded in what actually led to advancement — not just skill overlap.

Semantic Search & Dimensionality

PCA compresses 3,072-dim vectors to 1,536 for fast pgvector queries. Every match gets a precise cosine score and a specific reason code — not a black-box percentage.

Dual LLM Validation

Every extracted skill passes through two independent models: one extracts with a supporting quote, the second independently validates the skill-quote pair. No hallucinated skills get in.

Multi-Step Trajectory Analysis

The roadmap engine computes paths up to three promotions forward, auto-detecting “walls” where match strength falls below 50%. Full visibility into long-term trade-offs.

Behavioral Success Modeling

The system analyzes six categories of real performance data to determine what actually drives promotions: financial performance, compliance track record, work quality, professional development activity, people leadership, and NLP-analyzed themes from peer feedback. From this, it builds concrete benchmarks per level. For example, employees who reached Manager typically had 87% utilization and mentored at least 2 people. Recommendations are grounded in patterns that actually led to advancement, not just skill overlap.

Semantic Search & Dimensionality

PCA compresses those 3,072-dimensional vectors down to 1,536 dimensions for efficient storage and querying in pgvector without losing meaningful signal. The system measures the distance between skill vectors using cosine similarity, giving every match a precise score and a specific reason code rather than a black-box percentage.

Dual LLM Validation

Every skill on a user's profile passes through two independent AI models: the first reads the resume and extracts skills, but is required to attach the exact sentence that supports each claim. The second model independently reviews each skill-and-quote pair and rejects anything the quote doesn't actually prove. Two models checking each other means no hallucinated or unsupported skills make it into the system.

Multi-Step Trajectory Analysis

The roadmap engine computes career paths up to three promotions forward, with automatic detection of “walls” where match strength drops below 50%. Users see side-by-side path comparisons showing where each option leads and where it dead-ends. One path might show a strong fit all the way to Partner, while another offers easy entry but stalls two levels up. The goal is full visibility into long-term trade-offs before committing to a move.

Modular Enterprise Deployment

Vision

The platform is already built as independent feature modules: matching, skill extraction, roadmaps, and success patterns, each operating as its own service. Architecture is designed for selective deployment — organizations adopt only the modules their talent strategy requires. Pre-built integration points connect to existing HRIS, ATS, and LMS systems without replacing the tools teams already use.

Why this wins

Gartner predicts organizations adopting composable architectures will outpace competitors by 80% in new-feature implementation by 2026. SkillBridge has already built this way for EY; the same design makes it deployable elsewhere. Ease of integration is now the #1 decision factor for new HR-tech investments, overtaking price and features (Sapient Insights, 1,900 U.S. enterprises).

13.b

Portfolio Integration

Vision

We already extract and categorize certifications from resumes alongside technical, soft, and domain skills. Next: direct integration with enterprise credential platforms (Credly, internal LMS) so verified badges feed the matching algorithm automatically. Recommended skills become trackable credentials, closing the loop between “what should I learn” and “proof that I did”.

Why this wins

The digital-credential market is projected to grow from $2.1B to $7.8B by 2035, and 71% of Fortune 1000 companies run 15+ HR apps with fragmented credential data. Gartner's 2025 Hype Cycle lists AI-enabled skills management as high-benefit. Verified credentials in our matching algorithm moves us from inference to proof.

13.c

Human-Centered Governance

Vision

After a role is filled, hiring managers can retrospectively review audit trails for each candidate to identify bias patterns. The AI warns when recommendations skew for demographics — released only upon candidate permission. Every recommendation traces back to specific sources; the AI formats reasoning, the human gets the final decision.

Why this wins

The EU AI Act (Aug 2026) classifies AI hiring as high-risk and mandates audit logs for algorithmic decisions. NYC Local Law 144 already requires annual bias audits of automated hiring tools, with NJ / PA / CA advancing similar laws. The governance layer we're building anticipates the regulatory wave.
