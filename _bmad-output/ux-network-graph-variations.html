Network Graph Visualization - Design Variations

Traditional top-down tree structure with clear parent-child relationships. Easy to understand hierarchy, but can become wide with many nodes.

Variation 2: Force-Directed Organic Layout

Natural, organic arrangement where nodes repel each other but are pulled together by connections. Creates visually interesting, non-linear layouts that feel dynamic.

Center-focused radial layout with nodes arranged in concentric circles. Emphasizes the central role and creates clear visual hierarchy from center outward.
